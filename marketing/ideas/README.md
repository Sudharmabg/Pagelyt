# Idea batches

The social agent (`/pagelyt-social ideas`) writes one file per research run: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Approve ideas by replying in the session (for example "approve 2 and 5, make 5 a carousel"), or by changing `status:` here and asking the agent to create them.

## Template

```markdown
# Ideas: 2026-11-24

Research window: 2026-10-27 → 2026-11-24

## 1. <Hook, as it would appear on screen>
- **status:** proposed | approved | rejected | later | made → marketing/posts/<slug>/
- **Why now:** <the trend or news in one or two lines> ([source](https://…), [source](https://…))
- **Audience:** small business | freelancers & agencies | developers & marketers
- **Platforms and format:** Instagram reel (silent, 20 s) + LinkedIn video (captions) | carousel | document …
- **Pagelyt angle:** <the feature, tool or guide shown, e.g. /tools/ai-bot-checker>, and the screenshots needed
- **Outline:** 1) hook … 2) … 3) … 4) CTA
- **CTA:** link in bio | comment KEYWORD → DM <guide URL> | first-comment links
- **Effort:** S (reuse shots) | M (new screenshots) | L (new footage or voice)
```
