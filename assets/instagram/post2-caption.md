## Caption

3 SEO fixes you can do this week, no agency needed 👇

1️⃣ Titles people click: keep titles around 50–60 characters and descriptions under ~160. Lead with what people search for.
2️⃣ Lighter images: anything over 300 KB is slowing you down. Switch to WebP and lazy-load what's below the fold.
3️⃣ Let AI read your site: check robots.txt isn't blocking GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot, then add an llms.txt.
