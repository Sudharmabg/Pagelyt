## Caption

Most websites don't need an agency. They need an afternoon.

We see the same three problems again and again when we audit sites. Each one is quick to fix:

→ Titles people click. Keep titles around 50–60 characters and descriptions under ~160. Put what people search for first.
→ Lighter images. Anything over 300 KB slows the page down. Switch to WebP and lazy-load what's below the fold.
→ Let AI read your site. Check that robots.txt isn't blocking GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot, then add an llms.txt.

Swipe through for the checks and fixes, plus a checklist to save for later.

Free tools to check all three are in the first comment 👇

Which of the three is your site missing?
