01Titles & descriptions

Write titles people

Your title and meta description are your ad on Google. Weak ones get skipped, even if you rank.

Check

Title around 50–60 characters, description under ~160. One unique pair per page.

Fix

Lead with what people search for, then why they should click.

02Page speed

Shrink your images.
Watch pages fly.

Images are usually the heaviest thing on a page. Slow pages lose visitors and rankings.

Check

Flag any image over 300 KB, especially on your homepage.

Fix

Convert to WebP, resize to the size shown, and lazy-load images lower down.

03AI search

Let ChatGPT & co.
read your site.

AI tools send their own bots. One old line in robots.txt can block them, so they never recommend you.

Check

Your robots.txt lets in GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot.

Fix

Remove the blocking rule, then add an llms.txt: a short guide to your site for AI.
