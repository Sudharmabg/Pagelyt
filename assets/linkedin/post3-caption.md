## Caption

Ask ChatGPT for the best [your business type] near you. Is your business in the answer?

More people now ask AI tools instead of scrolling through Google. AI gives one answer and names just a few businesses. If it can't read your site, you're not one of them.

Three fixes to get into the answer:

→ Let the AI bots in. Make sure robots.txt doesn't block GPTBot, ClaudeBot or PerplexityBot. Security plugins often do it by accident.
→ Give AI a map. Add an llms.txt file that sums up who you are and links your key pages.
→ Answer questions plainly. Use question headings, put the answer in the first line, and add an FAQ.
