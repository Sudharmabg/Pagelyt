# Product Positioning – Pagelyt

Proposed Brand

Website Intelligence SaaS

## 1. Product identity

Product name: Pagelyt

Category: Website Intelligence & Optimisation Platform

Tagline: Illuminate Your Website.

Core positioning statement:

Pagelyt is an AI-powered website intelligence platform that helps businesses, website owners, freelancers and digital agencies understand, monitor and improve their websites through comprehensive analysis, actionable recommendations and continuous intelligence.

## 2. The problem we're solving

Most website owners rely on multiple disconnected tools to understand their website.

- Performance tools tell them how fast their website is.
- SEO tools identify search optimisation issues.
- Security tools identify vulnerabilities and configuration problems.
- Analytics tools show traffic and visitor behaviour.
- Uptime tools notify them when their website goes down.

But these tools rarely bring everything together into one understandable, actionable picture.

Pagelyt solves this by bringing website intelligence into one platform.

## 3. Our core value proposition

# One URL. Complete Website Intelligence.

Enter your website URL and discover what's working, what's broken, what needs improvement and where opportunities exist.

Analyse

Understand your website across multiple dimensions.

Discover

Identify issues, risks and growth opportunities.

Optimise

Get actionable, AI-powered recommendations.

Monitor

Track improvements and receive regular intelligence reports.

## 6. Business model

Pagelyt will follow a website-based annual subscription model.

| Plan         | Annual Price     | Positioning                                               |
| ------------ | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free         | ₹0               | Initial website intelligence                              |
| Essential    | ₹999 / website   | Website maintenance and basic monitoring                  |
| Professional | ₹2,499 / website | Advanced intelligence and one 30-minute consultation      |
| Business     | ₹4,999 / website | Comprehensive intelligence and one 60-minute consultation |

Additional revenue:

- One-time comprehensive website audit.
- Standalone one-on-one consultations.
- Website optimisation and implementation services.
- Additional competitor monitoring.

## 7. Key differentiators

Pagelyt should not compete purely on the number of metrics or features.

Its differentiation should come from:

1. URL-first experience: No complicated configuration required to start analysing a website.
2. Unified intelligence: Performance, SEO, GEO, security and accessibility in one place.
3. AI-powered recommendations: Explain problems and suggest practical fixes, prioritised by impact.
4. Continuous intelligence: Compare reports over time and understand what changed.
5. Business-friendly reporting: Make technical website problems understandable to nontechnical owners.
6. Human expertise: Customers can book consultations directly with you.
7. Agency-ready: Manage multiple websites and provide professional client reports.

## 8. Product experience

The intended customer journey:

1. Enter website URL

   Customer submits a website URL without complicated setup.
2. Automatic website analysis

   Pagelyt analyses health, performance, SEO, GEO, security and accessibility.
3. Receive intelligence report

   Customer sees scores, issues, evidence and recommendations.
4. Choose an annual plan

   Unlock advanced analysis, scheduled monitoring, historical comparisons and reporting.
5. Continuous improvement

   Receive monthly intelligence reports and track website improvements.
6. Book expert consultation

   Customers can discuss findings and improvement strategies directly with you.

## 10. What Pagelyt should become

My long-term vision would be:

Pagelyt is the intelligence layer for websites.

Not just another SEO checker. Not just another uptime monitor. Not just another performance dashboard.

A platform that continuously understands a website, identifies opportunities, explains problems and helps its owner make better decisions.

The important distinction is that the initial report can be generated from a URL, while deeper insights such as traffic, conversions and search rankings require customer integrations or tracking.

My recommendation: Keep this as our foundational product positioning. Build the first release around website analysis and actionable recommendations, then expand into continuous intelligence, agency workflows and advanced AI visibility.

# Roadmap Refinement (Decisions from Discovery Q&A)

## 11. Strategic decisions

| Area | Decision |
| ---- | -------- |
| Market | Global from day one |
| Wedge customer | Freelancers and agencies |
| Business role | Standalone SaaS (subscriptions are the main revenue; services are secondary) |
| Team | Solo builder |
| Timeline | 6 months to public v1 |
| Stack | Next.js + Postgres + queue workers (scan engine runs as a worker service) |
| Consultations | Keep bundled in Professional and Business as originally planned |
| Agency pricing | Volume tiers per website |
| Currency | Separate regional pricing (₹ for India, separate USD pricing for global) |
| Free tier | Email-gated full report with limited scans per day |
| Year-one metrics | Free scans run; free-to-paid conversion |

## 12. v1 module scope

Shipping in v1:

- Health + Performance (uptime, SSL, DNS, Lighthouse, Core Web Vitals)
- SEO + Security (technical SEO, TLS, security headers, mixed content)
- GEO readiness (AI crawler access, structured data, llms.txt)
- Accessibility (automated WCAG-oriented checks)

Deferred past v1:

- Content Intelligence
- Website Analytics (requires customer integrations)
- Competitor Intelligence
- AI website assistant

## 13. Positioning adjustments

- Lead with differentiators that are hard to copy: prioritised plain-English fixes, change tracking over time, business-friendly and white-label reports, agency workflows.
- Treat "URL-first" and "all-in-one" as table stakes, not headline differentiators.
- Frame GEO as **readiness** (measurable signals), not a ranking or visibility score.
- Primary messaging should speak to freelancers and agencies first ("audit clients faster, report professionally"), with small business owners as a secondary audience reached through them.

## 14. Pricing and business model updates

> **Status: pricing decisions are deferred.** All figures below are working assumptions, not commitments. Pricing will be finalised later, before Phase 4 (Accounts and monetisation).

- **Regional pricing:** keep ₹999 / ₹2,499 / ₹4,999 for India. Global USD prices are to be decided (see open questions).
- **Agency volume tiers:** per-website price decreases at higher site counts (proposed breakpoints: 5, 10, 25+ sites; exact discounts to be decided). White-label reports should be a feature of multi-site plans.
- **Consultations stay bundled.** Risk to monitor: a 60-minute session inside a ₹4,999 plan consumes much of the revenue and does not scale. Mitigations to consider: cap one session per plan per year, require booking via scheduled slots, and review margin after the first 50 paid sites.
- **Free tier:** email-gated full report, daily scan cap per email and IP, cached repeat scans to control cost.

## 15. Phased delivery plan (solo, 6 months)

A solo build of four modules plus billing, monitoring and white-label in six months is tight, so the plan is staged with checkpoints.

| Phase | Timing | Goal | Deliverables |
| ----- | ------ | ---- | ------------ |
| 0. Foundations | Weeks 1-3 | Skeleton and scan pipeline | Next.js app, Postgres schema, queue and worker, URL validation and rate limiting, deployment |
| 1. Scan engine MVP | Weeks 4-9 | Core analysis works | Health and Performance checks, SEO and Security checks, scoring model, stored scan results |
| 2. Report and funnel | Weeks 10-13 | Public free scan | Report UI, email gate, AI-prioritised fix list, shareable PDF, landing page |
| 3. GEO + Accessibility | Weeks 14-17 | Complete v1 module set | GEO readiness checks, accessibility checks, merged into scores and fix list |
| 4. Accounts and monetisation | Weeks 18-22 | Paid plans live | Auth, per-website plans, regional pricing, payments, rescans and diff view, scheduled monitoring and alerts |
| 5. Agency and launch | Weeks 23-26 | Agency-ready public launch | Multi-site dashboard, volume tiers, white-label PDF, consultation booking, monthly reports, public launch |

Suggested checkpoint: release Phase 2 as a soft launch (free scan only) to start measuring free scans and email capture while the rest is built.

## 16. Success metrics (year one)

- **Free scans run:** top-of-funnel volume.
- **Free-to-paid conversion:** share of email-captured users who buy a plan.

Secondary signals to track informally: paying websites, agency accounts, consultation bookings.

## 17. Key risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Scope too large for a solo builder | Phase gating; soft-launch the free scan early; cut Accessibility to a later phase if behind |
| Consultations eat margin | Cap sessions, schedule slots, review margin early |
| Scanning and AI costs on a free tier | Rate limits, email gate, caching, scan queue limits |
| Crowded market (SEOptimer, Sitechecker, Semrush audits) | Compete on fix prioritisation, change tracking and agency reporting, not metric count |
| Unfalsifiable GEO claims | Report only measurable readiness signals |
| Global launch complexity (tax, payments, currency) | Use a merchant-of-record or established payment provider; confirm approach before Phase 4 |

## 18. Open questions

1. Global USD prices for Essential, Professional and Business.
2. Exact agency discount breakpoints and white-label inclusion.
3. Scan frequency for monitoring on each plan (daily, weekly, monthly).
4. Payment and tax approach for global sales (e.g. merchant of record vs direct gateway).
5. Which AI model and budget per scan for recommendations.
6. Free-tier limits (scans per day per email and per IP).
7. Whether Accessibility is a hard v1 requirement or the first item to cut if the schedule slips.
